# Hooks for audio podcasts

Audio has no thumbnail and no on-screen text. The hook is the first 5–30 seconds the
listener *hears*, so it has to work with voice alone. Use this to judge candidates
from `hook_candidates.py` and to build the cold open and clips.

## The test (every hook must pass all four)

1. **Zero-context:** someone who never heard the show understands it. No "as I said", "that guy", "this thing".
2. **Open loop:** it raises a question, tension or stake that is *not* resolved inside the hook.
3. **Specific:** a number, a name, a concrete situation beats a generality ("we lost 40k in a week" > "we had problems").
4. **Relevant now:** it touches something from `trends_brief.md` — or the audience's exact phrasing of a pain/desire.

If two candidates tie, prefer: higher vocal energy → a guest over the host → the one that ends on a rising line.

## Hook archetypes (with how to cut them)

| Archetype | What it sounds like | How to cut |
|---|---|---|
| Contrarian claim | "Everyone says X. It's the worst advice in the industry." | End right after the claim, before the reasoning. |
| Unresolved story | "So I'm sitting in the meeting and the CEO says…" | Cut at the cliffhanger, before the outcome. |
| Shocking number | "73% of shows die before episode 7." | Number + one line of implication. |
| The question | "Why does nobody talk about…?" | Question + 1 sentence that sharpens it. |
| Confession / stakes | "I almost quit three times this year." | Keep the emotion; cut the explanation. |
| Prediction | "In 12 months this tool won't exist." | Claim + the boldest supporting line. |
| Trend reaction | "Everyone's arguing about [trend]. Here's what they miss." | Name the trend in the first 3 seconds. |
| Debate clash | Two voices disagreeing sharply | Back-and-forth, 2–4 turns, stop mid-argument. |

## Cold open recipe (before the intro music/host intro)

- 15–45 s total, 1–3 excerpts. Two excerpts that contrast (a claim, then a pushback) work well.
- Start on the strongest *word*, not on "so" / "yeah" / "I think". Trim the run-up.
- End on tension. Then: sting → "Welcome to …" → within 60 s say what the listener gets.
- The cold-open material should still appear later in the episode — the listener gets the payoff there.

## Clips for socials (audiograms, Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts audio, LinkedIn)

- 20–60 s (up to 90 s for LinkedIn/YouTube). One idea per clip.
- First 3 s = the hook line. Cut any warm-up even if it's only a second.
- End on a complete thought or a punchline; fade out 0.6 s.
- Cover every speaker across the clip set. Avoid two clips that make the same point.
- Clips are mastered at −14 LUFS by default (louder social platforms); episode at −16.

## Titles & captions (from the trends brief)

- Lead with the trend term or audience phrasing, then the twist: "[Trend]: why [surprising angle]".
- Keep the promise the cold open makes. No bait the episode doesn't pay off.
- Write 3 options: one curiosity, one number/specific, one contrarian.

## Things that look like hooks but aren't

- Inside jokes and references to earlier episodes.
- Long setups ("So, to give some context…").
- Lines that only land with a visual ("look at this chart").
- Hot takes that the speaker walks back two sentences later — check what follows.
